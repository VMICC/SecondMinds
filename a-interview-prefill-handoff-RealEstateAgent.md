# Interview Pre-Fill, Real Estate Agent Edition: Hand This File to an AI That Already Knows You

**This edition is for licensed real estate agents only.** It matches the Simple Second Mind v3.1-RE build. Loan officers and other business owners have their own editions of this handoff (LO and BIZ); do not use this one for them, the questions and the compliance rules are different.

**For the owner:** paste this whole file into a fresh chat with an AI you have worked with for a long time (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini, any of them), one that has memory of you and your business. It will fill in the interview below from what it already knows. Save what it gives back as a markdown file and drop that file into the `raw/` folder of your Simple Second Mind (the v3.1-RE build). The system there treats every answer in it as imported and not yet verified: it will show each one back to you for a quick confirm or correct, ask only what is still missing, and then flesh the thin spots out with you. The hours that AI spent learning you become your head start instead of something you retype.

Everything below the line is written to the AI, not to you.

---

## Instructions to the AI reading this

You are pre-filling a setup interview for a second brain belonging to a licensed real estate agent you already know (the real estate agent edition, v3.1-RE) and have worked with, possibly for years. Your memory of this person, your past conversations with them, and anything they attach to this chat are your only sources.

**Why honesty matters here:** every answer you write will be verified with the owner, one short confirm at a time. An answer you actually know saves them a question. A blank costs them nothing, they just get asked. A guess costs them the most, because they have to catch it and correct it. So: a blank is helpful, a guess is expensive.

### The rules

1. **Answer only what you genuinely know** about this specific person from your work with them. Not what is typical for real estate agents, not what would be plausible, not what fills the space. If you do not know, leave the answer line empty and move on.
2. **Tag every answer** at the end of its line with one of these:
   - `[from memory]` you know this because the owner told you or it is in your saved memory of them. Add a short note of where it came from if you can ("from their bio project," "from listing work in 2025").
   - `[proposed]` this is your inference or your draft, built from things you do know. Proposals are welcome where the interview asks for generation (client profiles, the differentiator, content angles), and they are labelled so the owner can correct them in one pass.
   - Never tag an inference `[from memory]`.
3. **Numbers are sacred.** Homes sold, years licensed, prices, percentages, results: write one only if the owner actually gave you that number. Never round it, estimate it, or carry one over from a draft you once wrote for them that they never confirmed.
4. **Stories and quotes must be real.** A story goes in only if the owner actually told you that story. A client quote goes in only in words a client actually used, as the owner reported them. Paraphrase is fine for stories; invented detail is not. If you embellish, you have made the owner's verification harder, not easier.
5. **Credentials and designations:** name only ones you know the owner holds. "None that I know of" and a blank are both acceptable; an assumed designation is not.
6. **Competitors:** name only ones the owner has actually mentioned losing business to, and only what the owner said they do well.
7. **Use the owner's own words** wherever you have them: their phrases, their town names, their way of describing a client. Their exact wording is worth more than your polished version.
8. **Formatting:** return the whole questionnaire below as one markdown document, same headings, same question numbers, with your answers filled in under each question and the rest of your reply kept out of it. No em dashes anywhere. Name the file `interview-prefill.md` if your platform lets you name downloads.
9. **End the document** with the two lists specified at the bottom: what you could not answer, and what you proposed that needs the owner's correction. Be complete in those lists; they are the owner's agenda for finishing the setup.
10. **If you know almost nothing** about this owner, say so at the top of the document and answer only what you do know. A short honest document beats a full invented one, every time.
